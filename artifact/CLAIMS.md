# SCARF Artifact Claim Contract

This document is the source of truth for the results claimed during MICRO 2026
artifact evaluation. A result is reproduced only when the command below
finishes successfully and `scripts/run_ae.py validate` reports `PASS`.

The active claim set is recorded in `artifact/claim_status.json`. Dataset and
checkpoint recovery succeeded, but real TranSplat and MVSplat probes showed
that the paper's sparse SAES quality/protocol contract is not reproduced by the
available implementation. Table 1 and Tables 2--3 are therefore not claimed.
DL3DV is additionally unavailable, and Figure 8 has no real Orin NX evidence.
These states never fall back to manuscript numbers or to the dense diagnostic.

## Declared Functional Checks

These checks validate public artifact components. They are not presented as a
reproduction of the paper's unavailable sparse-SAES or commercial-TSMC28
results and do not support a Results Reproduced badge request by themselves.

## Not Claimed as Exactly Reproduced

The TSMC 28 nm absolute values in Figure 9 and Table 4 are comparison targets,
not results regenerated by the public artifact. The commercial process design
kit, memory compiler, and Liberty collateral cannot be redistributed. The
artifact instead exposes the complete ASAP7 predictive implementation and a
deterministic DeepScaleTool normalization. See `artifact/HARDWARE_SCOPE.md`.

## Quality Terminology

The 0.072% statement in the paper is a maximum PSNR *degradation*, not a maximum
absolute PSNR change. A positive change such as the DepthSplat/DL3DV value may
exceed 0.072% without contradicting that degradation bound. Result files store
signed change, degradation, and absolute change separately.

## Validation States

- `PASS`: required inputs exist and all numeric/structural checks pass.
- `FAIL`: the experiment ran but a required output or tolerance failed.
- `NOT_RUN`: no result was supplied to the validator.
- `NOT_CLAIMED`: the value is reported for context but is outside the claim.

Missing data, checkpoints, cycle counts, or physical-design reports never
silently fall back to manuscript constants.

The public LPDDR5 Ramulator and DRAMPower smoke workflow is Functional evidence.
It is not claimed as a reproduction of the LPDDR4X timing or energy in the paper.
